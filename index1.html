ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY

Life at CCU

National Chung Cheng University

National Chung Cheng University (CCU) is located in Minxiong Township, Chiayi County, Taiwan. The university provides an environment where students can combine academic learning, research, technology and campus life.

The campus is surrounded by green spaces, trees and open landscapes. One of the well-known locations on campus is Tranquility Lake, which provides students with a peaceful place to walk, relax and spend time with friends.

CCU has colleges covering areas including humanities, science, engineering, law, social sciences, management and education. This makes the university a diverse environment for students from different academic backgrounds.

For international and exchange students, CCU also provides opportunities to experience Taiwanese culture, interact with students from different countries and participate in academic and extracurricular activities. The campus combines a traditional university atmosphere with modern education and research.
