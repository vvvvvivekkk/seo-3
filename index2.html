ABOUT STUDENT CONNECT

One place for student life

Connecting students across cultures

Student life is more than attending classes. University is also a place where people meet new friends, exchange ideas, discover different cultures and create memories together.

Student Connect is a website concept designed around this idea. It provides a simple digital space where students can discover activities, meet other students and learn about opportunities available around their university community.

For exchange and international students, connecting with people can be especially useful. A new university and a new country can feel unfamiliar during the first few weeks. Finding classmates, student groups and people with similar interests can make the transition easier.

Students can use the platform to discover campus activities, cultural events, study groups, technology communities, sports activities and informal meetups. The goal is not to replace existing university services, but to create a simple student-focused space for discovering and sharing experiences.

At CCU, students from different academic backgrounds and countries can bring different perspectives to the same community. Student Connect aims to turn those differences into opportunities for collaboration, friendship and learning.
